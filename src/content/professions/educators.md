  A working educator, teacher, professor, or trainer, can go from idea to published book in 6 to 12 months

  The writing is not the hard part. Fitting it around grading, terms, and a life outside the classroom is.

obstacles:
  - objection: "Between terms, grading, and everything else, I have no time left."
    reframe: >-
      True, and the educators on our roster didn't have spare time either. The manuscript is extracted in
      structured sessions built around the school calendar, breaks included, your talking, our system doing
      the assembly.
  - objection: "There are already a lot of education books out there."
    reframe: >-
      Most are broad. Your specific grade level, your specific subject, your specific method is the material
      competitors can't copy. Positioning finds that angle before you draft a page.
  - objection: "I'm not sure my classroom experience translates outside the school."
    reframe: >-
      It usually translates further than teachers expect. Parents, other teachers, and even businesses want
      what you already know about how people actually learn. Positioning finds that wider reader.
  - objection: "Editors will make it sound like a textbook, not like me."
    reframe: >-
      Your voice survives editing here. We edit with you, not over you, and the way you actually talk to a
      room of students stays on the page.

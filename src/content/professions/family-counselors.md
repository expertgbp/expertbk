obstacles:
  - objection: "Confidentiality makes storytelling almost impossible."
    reframe: >-
      It should make you cautious, and it doesn't have to make the book impossible. Every example is
      built as a composite drawn from patterns across many families, with identifying details removed
      before a word reaches the page. This is handled explicitly in editing, with your review at every
      step.
  - objection: "The topics are heavy; who wants to read that?"
    reframe: >-
      A family living through what you write about wants exactly that. They're not looking for something
      light. They're looking for someone who understands what they're carrying and can describe a way
      through it in plain language.
  - objection: "My licensing body has rules about public writing."
    reframe: >-
      That's worth taking seriously, and it's also manageable. Positioning and editing are built to
      respect scope-of-practice and public-statement guidelines for your license, and you review every
      word before it's published. Nothing goes out that you wouldn't stand behind professionally.

    reframe: >-
      True, and the HR leaders on our roster didn't have spare hours either. The manuscript is extracted in

    reframe: >-
      Yes, the same way you'd present a case study internally: details changed or combined so the lesson
      holds and no one is identifiable. This is handled explicitly in editing.
  - objection: "Editors will make it sound like a corporate memo."
    reframe: >-
      Your voice survives editing here. We edit with you, not over you, and the plain, direct language
      that gets a room to listen stays on the page.

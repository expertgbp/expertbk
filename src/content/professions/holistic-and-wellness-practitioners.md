    reframe: >-
      That's true of almost every hands-on practice before it's written down. The extraction sessions

      through it in the room, not to compress years of training into a paragraph.
  - objection: "I don't want to sound like every wellness influencer."
    reframe: >-
      Fair, and it's the most common practitioner pain we see. We edit with you, not over you, so the
      book sounds like the practitioner people already trust in person, not a template with your name
      swapped in.

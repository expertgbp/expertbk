obstacles:
  - objection: "Dating advice is a crowded, noisy space."
    reframe: >-
      It's crowded with generic advice and thin on anyone willing to be specific. Positioning starts with
      the one client and the one pattern you're best at fixing, not with out-shouting every other book on
      the shelf.
  - objection: "I don't want to be lumped in with pickup-artist books."
    reframe: >-
      Fair, and worth being deliberate about. Positioning sets the tone and the promise on page one, so
      the book reads like respect and skill instead of tactics on someone. Editing keeps that distinction
      consistent through the whole manuscript.
  - objection: "My clients are private about needing help."
    reframe: >-
      Which is exactly why the book matters: it lets someone learn your approach and trust your voice
      before they ever have to admit anything to a person. Composite examples protect every real client
      the same way they do in any coaching practice.

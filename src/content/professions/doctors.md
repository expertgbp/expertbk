  - objection: "I don't have time to write."

    reframe: >-
      The most common physician pain we see. Your reader may be exhausted, scared, and looking for one

      what stays in the clinic.

    reframe: >-
      Patient privacy, claims discipline, and scope-of-practice language are handled in editing by design.
      You review every word. Nothing ships that you wouldn't defend in front of a colleague.
  - objection: "Editors will make me sound like everyone else."
    reframe: >-
      Your voice survives editing here. We edit with you, not over you, and your teaching vocabulary stays
      yours.

obstacles:
  - objection: "Relationship advice is everywhere; why would mine matter?"
    reframe: >-
      The market isn't short on advice. It's short on a voice one specific kind of couple actually trusts.
      Positioning starts with the one relationship pattern you fix better than almost anyone, not with
      out-talking a shelf of generic books.
  - objection: "I can't share client stories."
    reframe: >-
      You're right, and you shouldn't. Every example in the book is built as a composite: the pattern
      stays true, the identifying details don't exist. This is handled explicitly in editing, the same
      way it's handled in a session note.
  - objection: "I'm afraid of sounding preachy."
    reframe: >-
      Preachy comes from telling. Your best sessions aren't lectures, they're questions that get a couple
      to their own answer. The manuscript is extracted the same way, so the book reads like your actual
      coaching voice, not a relationship-advice-column voice standing in for it.

Most people who come to us with "I want to write a book" don't actually need to sit down and write. They
need a way to get twenty or thirty years of knowledge out of their head in an order that makes sense. That
is what structured extraction is for.

This is the Produce stage of the EPIC Publishing Path, but it starts with Engineer: no session happens
until your positioning is locked. A well-structured book on the wrong topic is still the wrong book.
